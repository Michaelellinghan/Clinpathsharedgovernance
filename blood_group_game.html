How to Interpret Blood Group Reactions

Forward Group (Cells + Antibody Reagents)

  • • Positive (+) = Agglutination = Antigen PRESENT on red cells
  • • Negative (−) = No agglutination = Antigen ABSENT
  • • Anti-A + : Group A or AB antigen on cells
  • • Anti-B + : Group B or AB antigen on cells
  • • Anti-D + : D (Rh) antigen present → Rh positive

Reverse Group (Serum + Known Cells)

  • • A₁ cells + : Anti-A present in serum → NOT group A or AB
  • • B cells + : Anti-B present in serum → NOT group B or AB
  • • O cells − : Should always be negative (negative control)
  • • Reverse confirms forward — discrepancies require investigation
  • • Reverse group not valid in neonates (no natural antibodies yet)
