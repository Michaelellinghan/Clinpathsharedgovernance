🧬 FFP / Plasma

FFP contains plasma antibodies. Donor antibodies must NOT react with patient's red cells. Rh compatibility NOT required for FFP.

PatientCan Receive

For FFP, O is the universal RECIPIENT (not donor). AB is the universal DONOR.

🩺 Platelets

ABO-compatible preferred (small plasma volume). Rh-compatible preferred especially in women of childbearing age (D antigen on contaminating RBCs). Anti-D prophylaxis if Rh+ platelets given to Rh− patient.

PriorityRule
